Comprehensive Guide

Simple steps to Perfect Submissions.

Master PaperClip in minutes. Our intuitive workflow is designed to save you time and ensure your university documents are perfectly formatted and ready for BLC.

Step 01

Configure Metadata

Input your academic credentials, course details, and instructor information. Choose your document type to load the correct DIU template. For Presentations, toggle between Single or Multi mode to support groups of up to 8 members.

Step 02

Asset Orchestration

Drag and drop your images or existing PDFs into the secure upload zone. Use the interactive grid to reorder pages with precision.

Step 03

One-Click Synthesis

Initiate the generation process. Our client-side engine will merge your assets and the cover page into a single, high-resolution PDF stream.

Step 04

Preview & Export

Review the generated document in real-time. Once verified, export your professional PDF directly to your local device storage.

Step 05

AI Academic Analysis

Navigate to the Analyze page, copy the specialized analysis command, and use one of our integrated AI services to get a professional rating and feedback on your document.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our privacy-first PDF generation platform.

Supported Formats

We support high-resolution JPG, PNG, and PDF files. You can mix and match formats seamlessly in a single document.

Group Support

Our Presentation mode supports teams of up to 8 students. Simply switch to 'Multi' mode to generate a group cover page with everyone's details.

Smart Compression

Our engine automatically optimizes image assets to ensure your final PDF is compact enough for BLC uploads without losing quality.

AI Integration

Our Analysis tool provides pre-optimized prompts for third-party AI services, allowing you to get instant feedback on your assignments with a simple copy-paste workflow.

Data Sovereignty

Your documents never leave your browser. All processing is 100% local, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Cross-Platform

Built with a mobile-first philosophy, PaperClip works flawlessly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop workstations.
